Privacy Policy

FieldStamp GPS Camera
Effective Date: 2026-07-03

FieldStamp GPS Camera is designed to help you capture GPS-stamped photos, organize projects, and create reports directly on your device. We aim to keep the app simple, useful, and respectful of your privacy.

Most of your data is stored locally on your device. Data is only sent outside your device when you choose to share or export it through another app or service.

1. Information We May Use

Depending on how you use the app, we may process the following types of information:

  • Photos you take inside the app
  • GPS location, coordinates, altitude, heading, and nearby address details when you allow location access
  • Notes, project names, project codes, and custom fields you enter
  • App settings such as language, units, date format, coordinate format, and watermark preferences
  • Local app state and usage-related settings stored on your device

2. How We Use Information

We use this information to:

  • Capture photos with GPS stamps
  • Display location details on photos and watermarks
  • Organize photos by project
  • Generate PDF reports
  • Save your preferences on your device
  • Show advertising in the free version of the app

3. Camera and Location Permissions

The app requests camera access so you can take photos and location access so it can capture GPS coordinates when you shoot. If you do not allow location access, some GPS-related features will not work properly.

4. Local Storage

Your projects, photos, notes, and settings are stored locally on your device so you can use the app even when you are offline.

5. Sharing and Exporting

When you export a PDF or share a photo, the data is sent to the app or service you choose. We do not control how third-party apps or services handle information after you share it.

6. Advertising

The free version of FieldStamp GPS Camera may display advertisements. Advertising services may process certain device and usage information according to their own privacy practices.

7. Third-Party Services

The app may use the following third-party services or system frameworks:

  • Google Mobile Ads for advertising in the free app
  • Apple system services for camera, location, photo library, PDF generation, and sharing

These third parties have their own privacy policies and practices. We encourage you to review them if you use those services.

8. Data Retention

We keep your data on your device for as long as it remains in the app or on your device. You can remove data by deleting photos, projects, or by uninstalling the app.

9. Deleting Your Data

You can delete your data by:

  • Deleting individual photos
  • Deleting projects and their related photos
  • Clearing app data from your device settings
  • Uninstalling the app

10. Security

We use reasonable measures to help protect the information stored in the app. However, no method of storage or transfer is completely secure, especially when you choose to share files with third-party services.

11. Children’s Privacy

FieldStamp GPS Camera is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in a way that would require separate child-specific handling.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the app, legal requirements, or our practices. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
