Privacy Policy

Quick Image PDF Resizer · by Nexa · Last updated: 11 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Nexa (“we”, “us”) handles information when you use the Quick Image PDF Resizer app (“the app”) on iOS and Android. We built the app so that your files stay with you.

In short
  • Your images, PDFs, scans and text are processed on your device. We never upload them to our servers.
  • We do not show ads and do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not ask for your name, email or an account.
  • We only receive limited, non-identifying data needed to process purchases.

1. Your files

All tools in the app (resize, compress, convert, remove background, scan, image to PDF, PDF to image, merge and split, text to PDF, PDF to Word, extract images, protect and unlock PDF) run locally on your device. The files you choose, the results you create and any passwords you type for protecting or unlocking PDFs are never sent to us or to anyone else.

The Remove Background tool uses an AI model. The first time you use it, the app downloads the model file from a public model host (Hugging Face) and keeps it on your device. Only the model is downloaded; your photos are not uploaded.

2. “My files” history

To let you find recent results again, the app keeps a copy of files you download in a private storage area inside the app on your device. This history never leaves your device. You can delete single items or clear everything from the Files screen, and it is removed when you uninstall the app.

3. Permissions and why we need them

PermissionWhy
CameraOnly when you tap “Take photo”, to scan documents or capture a photo to turn into a PDF. Photos stay on your device.
Photos / files pickerTo let you choose the images or PDFs you want to process. The app only sees the items you pick.
Save to Downloads / PhotosTo save the results you create when you tap Download or Save.
InternetTo load the app interface, download the background-removal model and process purchases.

You can turn these permissions off at any time in your device settings. Some features will then stop working.

4. Subscriptions and purchases

Pro purchases are handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to manage purchases and check whether Pro is active. RevenueCat receives a random, anonymous app user ID, your purchase and subscription status, the product you bought, your country/store region, and basic device information (such as OS version and app version). We do not receive your payment card details, your name or your Apple/Google account email.

5. Settings stored on your device

The app stores small preferences on your device, such as your chosen language and recently used tools. These are not sent to us.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We share data only with the service providers listed above (Apple, Google, RevenueCat) to run the app, or when required by law. When you use the Share button, the file goes to the app you choose, under that app’s own privacy policy.

7. Data retention

Your files and history stay on your device until you delete them or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as long as needed to provide your purchases and meet legal and tax obligations.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your history in the Files screen, or uninstall the app to remove all local data.
  • Turn off Camera or Photos access in your device settings.
  • Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the EU/UK, the CCPA in California, the LGPD in Brazil or Vietnam’s personal data protection rules), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data about you. Because we do not hold your files or an account, we usually have no personal data to return; contact us and we will help with anything held by our providers.

9. Security

Processing your files on your device is the strongest protection we can offer: there is no copy on our servers to leak. Connections to our providers use encryption (HTTPS). No method is 100% secure, but we work to keep the app safe.

10. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the new version on this page and update the “Last updated” date above.
