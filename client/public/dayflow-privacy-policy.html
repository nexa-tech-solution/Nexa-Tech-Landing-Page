Privacy Policy

Dayflow: ADHD Visual Planner · Nexa · Effective date: November 10, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Dayflow ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our visual day planner on iPhone, iPad, and Android. We built Dayflow to collect as little as possible: there is no account, no advertising, and no tracking. Your plan stays on your device.

Short version: no sign-up, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Everything you enter in Dayflow is stored on your device, encrypted, and is never uploaded to our servers.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

Dayflow saves the following on your device so the app can work:

  • Your plan: activities, times, durations, colours, icons, repeating activities, routines and their steps, and your Inbox.
  • Things you choose to record: energy check-ins (low, okay, good), whether an activity is marked as essential, and travel or get-ready times.
  • Time learning: how long you planned for an activity and how long it took when you used the focus timer, so the app can suggest better estimates.
  • Setup answers and settings: your usual wake-up and bedtime, what you find hardest, language, appearance, reminder preferences, and quiet hours.
  • A short history of completed activities (kept for up to 90 days) and the days you opened the app, used only for your weekly look-back.

This information is kept in the app's private storage and is encrypted with AES-256. The encryption key is created on your device and kept in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore. We do not upload this data, and we cannot see it. Some of it, such as medication reminders or energy levels, may be sensitive to you; it never leaves your device.

2. Calendar Access (Optional)

If you turn on "Show my calendar", Dayflow asks for permission to read the calendars already on your device (for example, Apple, Google, or Outlook accounts you have added in your phone's settings). Events are read on your device only to display them on your timeline. Dayflow does not change your calendars, does not store a copy of your events, and does not send them anywhere. You can turn this off in the app or in your device settings at any time.

3. Notifications

If you turn on reminders, Dayflow asks for permission to send notifications. Reminders (such as "starting soon", "time to leave", or "time's up") are scheduled locally on your device. We do not use push notification servers, and no reminder content is sent to us.

4. Widgets, Live Activities, and Quick Add

To show your current and upcoming activities on your Home Screen, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, or Android widget, the app shares a copy of today's and tomorrow's activities with its own widget through storage on your device. Quick Add (from a widget, Control Center, or a Shortcut) saves your text directly into your Inbox on your device. None of this is uploaded.

5. Feedback and Bug Reports

If you choose to send feedback or report a bug, Dayflow opens your email app with a message addressed to us. Nothing is sent until you press send. You can choose to include basic technical details: app version, phone model, operating system, and the number of activities, routines, and Inbox items. We never include the content of your tasks or notes. If the app crashes, the bug report may also include the technical error message. We use your email only to reply to you and to improve the app.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
  • We do not show ads or use advertising identifiers.
  • We do not use analytics or tracking tools, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not sell or share your personal information.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, microphone, or location.

7. Children

Dayflow is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. You can edit or remove activities, routines, and Inbox items in the app at any time. Deleting the app removes all of its data from your device. Because the data is encrypted with a key tied to your device, it cannot be restored on a different device from a backup. Emails you send us are kept only as long as needed to help you, and you can ask us to delete them.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR, UK GDPR, or U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, correct, or delete your information. Since we do not hold your app data on our servers, you can manage it directly in the app. For anything you have emailed us, contact us and we will help.

10. Security

Your data is stored in the app's private storage and encrypted with AES-256, with the key protected by your device's Keychain or Keystore. No method of storage is perfectly secure, but keeping your data on your device and encrypted greatly reduces the risk.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes, for example if we add optional features such as subscriptions or account sync. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page and, for significant changes, let you know in the app.
