Privacy Policy

Coc: Classic Board Games · Nexa · Effective date: November 17, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Coc: Classic Board Games ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our board game app on iPhone, iPad, and Android. The app is built to play offline: there are no accounts, and every game, including the AI opponents, runs entirely on your device.

Short version: no sign-up, no personal profile on our servers. Your games, stats, and settings stay on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers depending on your choices.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • Profile: your nickname, avatar, ratings, stats, and match history.
  • Games: saved games in progress and unlocked content.
  • Settings: app language, sound, vibration, theme, and reminder preferences.

This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, according to your device settings.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example, a daily play reminder) directly on your device. Your device's time zone is used only so reminders arrive at the right local time. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in the app or in your device settings.

3. Downloading Content

The app may download extra content, such as game packs or themes, from public file hosts. Like any website, these services receive technical information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address and device type. We do not send them any personal information or game data.

4. In-App Purchases

Purchases (for example, removing ads or unlocking content) are processed entirely by the Apple App Store or Google Play. We never receive your payment details. The app only receives confirmation of what was purchased so it can unlock and restore it.

5. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads, including optional rewarded videos, provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, IDFA on iOS or the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions and basic app usage needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention

On iOS, the app asks for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK, and certain U.S. states), we show a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform before ads are loaded, and you can change your choices later from "Privacy options" in the app's Settings. To ask for fewer personalized ads, you can also limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for ads.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your games, stats, or profile.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

7. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting the app removes all app data. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests can be handled directly on your device or through Google's ad settings, but you can always contact us for help.

10. Security

Content is downloaded over secure HTTPS connections, and your data is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
