Privacy Policy

AI Lecture Recorder

Effective date: 11/10/2026
Contact: cs.nexatech@gmail.com

This Privacy Policy explains how AI Lecture Recorder ("we", "our", or "us") handles information when you use the AI Lecture Recorder mobile app ("App").

If you have any questions, contact us at cs.nexatech@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

We designed the App to be local-first. Your recordings and related content are stored on your device. We do not create user accounts and we do not run servers that store your recordings.

The App may handle the following information:

  • Voice recordings you create, and audio or video files you import
  • Bookmarks, labels, notes, folders, transcription text, and summaries associated with your recordings
  • App preferences, such as language and recording quality
  • Reward points, daily chest progress, and quest status, stored on your device
  • Your Groq API key, if you choose to use Groq transcription. It is stored only on your device.
  • Advertising and device information collected by Google AdMob, as described in Section 6

2. How We Use Information

We use the information in the App to:

  • Record, import, trim, and play audio
  • Save recordings, bookmarks, notes, transcriptions, and summaries on your device
  • Display your library and recording details
  • Provide transcription and summary features
  • Track reward points used for Lecture AI transcription
  • Show ads, including rewarded ads, that keep the App free
  • Remember your app preferences

3. Local Storage

Recordings, bookmarks, notes, transcription text, summaries, reward points, and app preferences are stored locally on your device using the App's storage features. We do not intentionally store your recordings on our servers.

4. Transcription and Summary Features

The App offers several ways to transcribe recordings:

  • Lecture AI (offline): transcription runs entirely on your device using open-source Whisper models. The model files are downloaded once from Hugging Face; your audio is never uploaded.
  • On-device transcription: uses your device's built-in speech recognition (Apple on iOS, Google on Android). When on-device recognition is not available for your language or device, the platform provider may process the audio on its servers under its own privacy policy.
  • Groq transcription (optional): Groq, Inc. (United States) is a third-party AI service that runs the Whisper speech-to-text model. This option is used only if you add your own Groq API key and choose Groq for a recording.

Data shared with a third-party AI service (Groq)

  • What is sent: the audio of the recording you choose to transcribe, the audio language you select, and your Groq API key (used to authorize the request). No name, email, contacts, location, advertising identifier, or other recordings are sent.
  • Who receives it: Groq, Inc. only. The data is sent directly from your device to Groq over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. It does not pass through our servers.
  • How we ask: before anything is sent, the App shows a permission prompt that explains this data sharing. Nothing is sent unless you tap Allow. If you tap Don't Allow, no data is shared and you can use the offline or on-device options instead.
  • How it is used: only to create and return the transcription text, which is saved on your device. We do not use it for advertising, and it is never shared with advertisers.
  • Withdrawing permission: you can withdraw permission anytime with Withdraw Groq consent in the App's Settings, or by removing your API key. After that, no audio is sent to Groq until you allow it again.
  • Protection: Groq processes the data under its own privacy policy, which provides the same or equal protection of user data as this policy.

AI Summary uses Apple Intelligence on supported devices and runs on your device.

5. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions:

  • Microphone, so you can record audio
  • Speech recognition, for on-device transcription
  • Photo library and camera, only when you choose to import or capture audio or video to transcribe
  • Notifications, if enabled
  • App Tracking Transparency (iOS), to ask whether ads may use your device's advertising identifier

You can deny any permission, but some features may not work without it. Denying tracking does not limit any App features.

6. Advertising

The App shows ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect information such as your device's advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android), IP address, device type, and how you interact with ads, to serve, measure, and personalize ads and to prevent fraud.

The App is rated for all ages, so ads are limited to content suitable for general audiences (Google's "G" ad content rating), and sensitive ad categories are blocked.

  • On iOS, the advertising identifier is used only if you allow tracking in the App Tracking Transparency prompt. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking.
  • On Android, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in your device settings.
  • Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), we ask for your consent before showing personalized ads.

Your recordings, transcripts, and notes are never shared with advertisers. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

We do not sell your personal information.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob, for advertising
  • Hugging Face, to download offline transcription models
  • Groq, Inc., for optional cloud transcription with your own API key and only after you allow it (see Section 4)
  • Apple and Google speech recognition, Apple Intelligence, and other platform services needed to run the App

These services have their own privacy policies and terms. We share data with them only as described in this policy, and we expect them to provide the same or equal protection of user data as stated in this policy.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your content is stored on your device, you can remove it by:

  • Deleting recordings or notes inside the App
  • Clearing the App's local storage
  • Uninstalling the App

If you delete the App, the locally stored content on that device may be removed.

9. Children’s Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

10. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information handled by the App. However, no method of storage or transmission is 100% secure.
