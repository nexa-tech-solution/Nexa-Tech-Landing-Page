Privacy Policy

War Territory · Nexa · Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how War Territory ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you play our strategy game on iPhone, iPad, and Android. The game is played offline: there are no accounts, and every campaign, battle, and AI opponent runs entirely on your device.

Short version: no sign-up, no personal profile on our servers. Your campaigns, progress, and settings stay on your device. We use Google Firebase for anonymous usage statistics and crash reports, and Google AdMob for ads, which may use device identifiers depending on your choices.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • Game progress: saved campaigns, unlocked maps, gems, generals, and achievements.
  • Settings: app language, sound, and reminder preferences.

This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and it is deleted when you delete the app. Your device's own backup (iCloud or Google) may include it, according to your device settings.

2. Notifications

If you allow notifications, the app schedules local reminders (for example, to return to your campaign) directly on your device. Your device's time zone is used only so reminders arrive at the right local time. On Android, the app may re-schedule reminders after your device restarts. Nothing is sent to us. You can turn reminders off in your device settings.

3. In-Game Currency

Gems and other rewards are earned by playing and exist only inside the game. The app does not offer in-app purchases and never asks for payment details.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The app is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob: a banner on the map selection screen and occasional full-screen ads between battles. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, IDFA on iOS or the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions and basic app usage needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention

On iOS, the app asks for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK, and Switzerland), we show a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform before any ads are loaded, and you can change your choices later with the privacy button on the map selection screen. You can also limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for ads.

5. Analytics and Crash Reports (Google Firebase)

To understand how the game is played and to fix bugs, the app uses Google Firebase services:

  • Firebase Analytics: game events such as screens opened, campaigns started and finished, and battles won or lost, together with an app-instance ID, device model, OS version, app version, language, and approximate country.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: when the app crashes or hits an error, a crash report with the stack trace, device model, OS version, and app state at the time of the crash.

This information is not linked to your name or contact details and is not used to track you across other companies' apps. Learn more in Privacy and Security in Firebase.

6. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your campaigns or game progress.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, location, camera, or microphone.

7. Children

The app is suitable for a general audience but is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting the app removes all app data from your device. Analytics and crash data are kept by Firebase for a limited period according to Google's retention settings, and advertising data according to Google's own policies.

9. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests can be handled directly on your device or through Google's ad settings, but you can always contact us for help.

10. Security

Your game data is stored in the app's private storage on your device, and the third-party services above transmit data over secure HTTPS connections.

11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
