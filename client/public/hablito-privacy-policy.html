Privacy Policy

Hablito: Learn Spanish · Nexa · Effective date: November 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Hablito ("the app", "we", "us") handles information when you use our Spanish-learning app on iPhone, iPad, and Android. We built Hablito to collect as little as possible: there are no accounts, and your learning progress stays on your device.

Short version: no sign-up, no personal profile on our servers. Your progress and voice practice stay on your device. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use device identifiers depending on your choices.

1. Information Stored Only on Your Device

  • Learning progress: completed lessons and conversations, streaks, weekly goals, game scores, and review cards.
  • Settings: your chosen app language and preferences.
  • Downloaded content: course levels, conversation packs, and language files you download.

This data is saved in a local database on your device. We do not upload it to our servers, and it is deleted when you delete the app.

2. Microphone and Speech Recognition

When you choose to speak an answer, Hablito asks for access to your microphone and speech recognition. Recognition runs on your device (we request on-device recognition), and your voice is not sent to us. If your device supports it, a short recording of your answer may be kept on your device so you can play it back; it is never uploaded by us. You can always tap an answer instead of speaking, and you can turn off microphone access at any time in your device settings.

3. Downloading Lessons and Languages

Course levels, conversation packs, and app language files are downloaded from public file hosts (GitHub and jsDelivr). Like any website, these services receive technical information needed to deliver the files, such as your IP address and device type. We do not send them any personal information or learning data.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Hablito is free and shows ads (banners, full-screen ads between lessons, and optional rewarded videos) provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information such as:

  • Device and advertising identifiers (for example, IDFA on iOS or the Android Advertising ID)
  • IP address and approximate location derived from it
  • Ad interactions and basic app usage needed for ad delivery and fraud prevention

On iOS, the app asks for permission (App Tracking Transparency) before any identifier is used to track you across other companies' apps and websites. If you decline, ads are still shown but are less personalized. Where required by law (for example, in the EEA, UK, and certain U.S. states), we show a consent message powered by Google's User Messaging Platform, and you can change your choices later. To ask for fewer ads, you can also limit ad tracking in your device settings.

Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information for ads.

5. What We Do Not Do

  • We do not require an account, email, or phone number.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • We do not upload your learning progress, voice, or recordings.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, or location.

6. Children

Hablito is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Because your learning data lives on your device, you control it. Deleting a downloaded level removes it, and deleting the app removes all app data. Information collected by Google for advertising is kept according to Google's own policies.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under the GDPR or U.S. state privacy laws such as the CCPA), you may have rights to access, delete, or object to the processing of your information, and to opt out of targeted advertising. Since we do not hold personal data on our servers, most requests can be handled directly on your device or through Google's ad settings, but you can always contact us for help.

9. Security

Content is downloaded over secure HTTPS connections, and your progress is stored in the app's private storage on your device.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app or the law changes. When we do, we will update the effective date at the top of this page.
